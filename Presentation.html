The Problem

Clinical answers, buried in fragmented evidence

Guidelines span hundreds of pages: ADA Standards of Care runs 100+ pages — too dense to search manually under time pressure.
Search lacks clinical grounding: Generic search results give no way to verify source provenance.
LLMs hallucinate references: Generic models fabricate citations and mix outdated guidance with current standards.
No safety boundaries: Nothing stops standard LLMs from generating unsafe or unverified medical advice.
Core Challenge

"There is no lightweight tool that retrieves, synthesizes, and cites authoritative diabetes evidence with verifiable provenance."

Our Solution

A RAG system with safety baked into the pipeline

Grounded Generation: Every answer traces back to specific chunks from ADA and NIDDK documents.
Safety-First Architecture: 8 refusal reasons, 5 post-generation verification checks, and regex risk detection.
Evidence Transparency: Per-statement citations, confidence scores, and an evidence drawer let clinicians verify claims.
Multi-Provider Failover: Automatic fallback across Gemini, Groq, and OpenRouter ensures uptime.

Not Just an AI Answer

Retrieval Engine & Pipeline

Hybrid retrieval combining lexical and semantic signals

Hybrid Mechanics

BM25 Okapi: Exact keyword matching for clinical terminology (e.g., HbA1c, FPG).

Dense Embeddings: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384-dim) for semantic similarity.

RRF Fusion: Combines scores with dense weight 0.6.

Parameters: Top-K = 8, similarity threshold = 0.35.

Query Processing & Intent

Intent Classification: Rule-based routing categorizes queries (diagnosis, testing, treatment).

Query Expansion: Maps clinical abbreviations (A1C, FPG) to canonical terms.

Section Boosting: Prioritizes chunks from relevant guideline sections.

Latency: Sub-100ms end-to-end retrieval performance.

Safety & Verification

Rigorous post-generation verification and safety guardrails

8 Refusal Reasons

Catches unsafe queries before generation: low_relevance, insufficient_evidence, provider_failure, medical_advice, emergency, verification_failed, no_safe_answer, technical_error.

5 Post-Generation Checks

Validates answers before delivery: Citation validity check, hallucination prevention, safety compliance, confidence alignment, and source consistency verification.

Multi-Provider Failover Chain: Gemini 2.0 Flash → Groq Llama 3.3 70B → OpenRouter GPT-OSS 20B
